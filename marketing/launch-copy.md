## LinkedIn (attach `verdict-card.png` or the video)

Most founders hear the hard questions for the first time in front of real investors.

I built a Claude Code skill that moves that earlier. You type /shark-tank and pitch. A Host runs the room. Five Shark sub-agents, each with a different lens, ask their questions, make offers or go out, and negotiate.

You get a verdict with a scorecard, the valuation check, and three fixes to make before the real pitch.

It runs a US panel and an India panel, and you can add seats with /newshark. It also installs for Codex, Copilot, Cursor and Gemini CLI.

Open source: github.com/sidrat2612/shark-tank-skill
