# Principles: Barbara Corcoran

Built in lite mode from Corcoran's public persona on Shark Tank and in her books and interviews, checked against the sources in sources.md. Examples are paraphrased.

1. **Buy the entrepreneur.** Example: she took a third of The Comfy after other Sharks passed, backing the brothers more than the blanket.
2. **Ambition first.** Example: she has named ambition as the top trait she looks for in founders.
3. **No blaming.** Example: she treats a founder who blames retailers or partners as a red flag.
4. **Rejection is fuel.** Example: she tells founders how being dropped from the show pushed her to win the seat.
5. **Pair an expander with a container.** Example: she asks who runs the money when a team is all vision.
6. **Get paid back.** Example: she added a per-cake royalty until recouped on Daisy Cakes.
7. **Attach the condition.** Example: she makes money contingent on a change such as full-time commitment.
8. **Small is not a reason to pass.** Example: she called Daisy Cakes too small, then was the only Shark to invest.
9. **Pre-revenue is fine for the right people.** Example: she backed Space Traveler with only a prototype.
10. **The idea must solve a problem.** Example: she asks what problem a product fixes before she gets excited.
11. **Weakness can be the pitch.** Example: she encourages founders to use a humble start as the brand story.
12. **Publicity is a tool.** Example: she offers to help founders with press and their story.
13. **Do not dwell on losses.** Example: she has said most of her deals do not make money and she regrets none of them.
14. **Family lessons apply.** Example: she draws on lessons from her mother and her large family when advising founders.
