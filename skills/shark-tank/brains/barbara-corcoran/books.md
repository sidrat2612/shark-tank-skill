# Books: Barbara Corcoran

Built in lite mode from public records of Corcoran's published writing, shows, and companies, checked against the sources in sources.md. Theses are in our words.

## Use What You've Got, and Other Business Lessons I Learned from My Mom (2003, with Bruce Littlefield)

- **Thesis:** the lessons that built a New York real estate firm came from a mother raising ten children with little money.
- **Key frameworks:** use what you've got, make noise, expanders and containers.

## Nextville: Amazing Places to Live Your Life (2008)

- **Thesis:** a guide to places to live for the next stage of life. Not a business book.
- **Key frameworks:** none used in this brain.

## Shark Tales: How I Turned 1,000 Dollars into a Billion Dollar Business (2011, with Bruce Littlefield)

- **Thesis:** a revised edition of her 2003 book, updated with lessons from Shark Tank.
- **Key frameworks:** rebound test, use what you've got, make noise.

## The Corcoran Group (company, from 1973, sold to NRT in 2001)

- **Thesis:** a brokerage built on hiring for traits, a strong sales culture, constant publicity, and an expander and container at the top.
- **Key frameworks:** expanders and containers, make noise, gut read in the first minute.

## Business Unusual with Barbara Corcoran (podcast, iHeartMedia, from 2018)

- **Thesis:** short weekly episodes of business advice for small business owners.
- **Key frameworks:** buy the entrepreneur, rebound test.

## Shark Tank (TV, ABC, Corcoran as a Shark from 2009)

- **Thesis:** the show is where her investing style is most visible: gut reads on founders, payback structures, and conditional offers.
- **Key frameworks:** buy the entrepreneur, payback first, the one-fix condition.
