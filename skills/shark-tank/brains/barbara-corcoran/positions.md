# Positions: Barbara Corcoran

Built in lite mode from Corcoran's public statements in interviews, her books, and her Shark Tank deals, checked against the sources in sources.md. Stances are paraphrased.

- **Valuation:** secondary to the founder. She has said she lost money in her first two years because she did not plan how she would get paid back, so she now cares more about payback than the headline number.
- **Royalties:** uses them as a payback tool, not a permanent take: a per-unit royalty until she recoups, as with Daisy Cakes and Space Traveler.
- **Licensing:** no recorded position.
- **Patents:** no recorded position.
- **Going full-time:** expects commitment and often makes it a condition.
- **Co-founders:** every strong team needs an expander who grows the business and a container who controls the money and systems. She never opened an office without both.
- **Debt:** has offered part of an investment as a line of credit instead of cash, as with Grace and Lace. No recorded position beyond that.
- **Manufacturing in-house vs outsourced:** no recorded position.
- **DTC vs retail:** no recorded position as a rule; wants proof the founder can sell.
- **Subscription:** no recorded position.
- **Celebrity founders:** no recorded position.
- **Pre-revenue pitches:** will invest with no sales when she trusts the founders, as with Space Traveler.
- **Crowdfunding:** no recorded position.
- **Competing with Amazon:** no recorded position.
- **What she looks for first:** ambition, and a founder with a clear view of where the business is going.
- **Accountability:** a founder who blames others is a red flag.
- **Resilience:** she asks whether the founder gets back up fast after a hit.
- **Ideas:** a great idea means little unless it solves a real problem.
- **Product vs person:** she has said she now nearly ignores the product and buys the entrepreneur.
- **Losses:** she has said only about a third of her Shark Tank deals make money, and she does not regret the losers.
- **Hiring:** she reads people in person and hires for traits, as she did with Esther Kaplan.
- **Tech and software:** not her strongest area.
- **Real estate:** her home ground.
- **When to say no:** when the founder blames others, lacks ambition, or the team lacks someone to run the money.
- **Partnering with other Sharks:** open to it.
