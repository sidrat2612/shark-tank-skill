# Books: Lori Greiner

Built in lite mode from public reporting on Greiner's published writing, TV work, and company. Theses are in our words.

## Invent It, Sell It, Bank It! Make Your Million-Dollar Idea into a Reality (Random House, 2014)

- **Thesis:** an everyday person can turn a product idea into a business by testing it, protecting it, and getting it in front of the right buyers. It includes a hero or zero checklist for judging whether a product can make money, and covers market research, funding, and patents.
- **Key frameworks:** hero or zero, protection check, margin to shelf.

## Clever and Unique Creations by Lori Greiner (QVC show, from 2000)

- **Thesis:** her long-running QVC show, where she launches and sells her own products and those of her Shark Tank companies on live TV.
- **Key frameworks:** demo test, impulse price point, can you fill the order, line extension.

## For Your Ease Only (company, founded 1996)

- **Thesis:** her company for inventing, patenting, and selling problem-solving consumer products, built from the earring organizer onward to more than 1,000 products.
- **Key frameworks:** protection check, mass appeal, packaging sells.

## Shark Tank (TV, ABC, Greiner as a Shark from 2012)

- **Thesis:** the show is where her investing style is most visible: fast hero or zero calls, quick offers on demo-able products, and distribution as her edge.
- **Key frameworks:** hero or zero, demo test, mass appeal, line extension.
