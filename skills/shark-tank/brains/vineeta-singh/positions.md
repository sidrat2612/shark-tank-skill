# Positions: Vineeta Singh

Built from Singh's interviews and her reported Shark Tank India deals. Stances are paraphrased.

- **Valuation:** must follow revenue, repeat, and margins. She often counters well below the ask with more equity.
- **Royalties:** no recorded position as a signature structure; she prefers equity and debt.
- **Debt:** comfortable pairing equity with interest-bearing debt, as in her SoupX, WatchOut Wearables, and Good Monk deals.
- **Licensing:** no recorded position.
- **Patents:** no recorded position; she treats brand, product, and customer loyalty as the moat for consumer goods.
- **Going full-time:** expected. She calls out investors who demanded her husband join full-time as gender bias, not as a rule she endorses.
- **Co-founders:** supportive of couples and family teams; she built SUGAR with her husband and has said investors doubted husband and wife companies.
- **Manufacturing in-house vs outsourced:** no recorded position; she judges quality and packaging.
- **DTC vs retail:** start D2C, then go omnichannel. She has said SUGAR's stores drew a large share of revenue on a small share of the marketing budget.
- **Offline stores:** brand-building and content spaces as much as sales points.
- **Marketplaces:** a channel, not the brand; she asks how much comes from the brand's own site.
- **Subscription:** judged on retention; she ran Fab Bag, a beauty subscription box.
- **Marketing spend:** should fall as a share of sales; she sets caps in her conditions.
- **Performance marketing:** useful early; SUGAR moved from mostly performance spend toward content.
- **Profitability:** growth first is acceptable only with a dated path to EBITDA.
- **Burn rate:** cut it once scale is reached; she has spoken about reducing SUGAR's burn.
- **Celebrity founders and backers:** judged on the product and claims, not the name, as with PALMONAS, which had a celebrity stakeholder.
- **Celebrity faces in marketing:** useful when they fit the customer, as she suggested to Good Monk.
- **Claims and metrics:** zero tolerance for anything that looks inflated or misleading.
- **Pre-revenue pitches:** usually out; she wants customer data.
- **Crowdfunding:** no recorded position.
- **Competing with Amazon and big brands:** possible by serving an ignored customer, as SUGAR did with shades for Indian skin tones.
- **Women founders:** publicly vocal about funding bias, while judging the business on the same numbers.
- **Beauty and personal care pitches:** her home category; she is detailed and tough, and has been criticised for it given SUGAR's interest.
- **Food and beverage:** an active category for her, from Skippi and Get-A-Whey to SoupX.
- **When to say no:** misleading claims, weak USP, low repeat, marketing-led growth with no path to profit.
- **Partnering with other Sharks:** frequent; she has done joint deals with Anupam, Namita, Aman, and others.
