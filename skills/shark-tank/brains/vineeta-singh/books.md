# Books: Vineeta Singh

Built from public reporting on Singh's companies and public appearances. Theses are in our words.

No books written by Vineeta Singh are recorded in this brain. Her best-known companies and public appearances are listed instead.

## SUGAR Cosmetics (company, 2015)

- **Thesis:** a beauty brand built for Indian women and Indian skin tones, grown online first and then into stores, can take on global brands.
- **Key frameworks:** repeat rate test, online to omnichannel path, marketing to sales ratio, path to EBITDA.

## Fab Bag (company, 2012)

- **Thesis:** a monthly beauty subscription box that introduces customers to products and teaches the brand what they want.
- **Key frameworks:** repeat rate test.

## Quetzal (company, 2007)

- **Thesis:** her first startup, a background verification service for recruiters, which failed.
- **Key frameworks:** founder endurance.

## Shark Tank India (TV, Sony, Singh as a Shark from 2021)

- **Thesis:** the show is where her investing style is most visible: claim checks, equity plus debt, and offers tied to metrics.
- **Key frameworks:** claim check, conditional offer, product, packaging, and USP test.

## Interviews and podcasts (various, from 2021)

- **Thesis:** founders win by staying close to customers, cutting burn at scale, and enduring years of setbacks. Appearances include Shantanu Deshpande's YouTube show and Ranveer Allahabadia's podcast.
- **Key frameworks:** founder endurance, path to EBITDA.
