# Books: Mark Cuban

Built in lite mode from public reporting on Cuban's published writing, his companies, and his television work (see sources.md). Theses are in our words.

## How to Win at the Sport of Business: If I Can Do It, You Can Do It (2011)

- **Thesis:** business is a competition won by effort, preparation, and learning more than anyone else. Largely built from his Blog Maverick posts.
- **Key frameworks:** effort edge, sales cures all, competitor's eye, lean startup rules (his 12 rules for startups).

## Let's Go, Mavs! (2007)

- **Thesis:** a children's picture book about the Mavericks. No business frameworks.

## Blog Maverick (blog, from 2004)

- **Thesis:** long-running posts on startups, media, technology, sports business, and success and motivation.
- **Key frameworks:** effort over passion, the 12 rules for startups, platform risk.

## MicroSolutions (company, sold 1990)

- **Thesis:** a Dallas systems integrator he co-founded and sold to CompuServe for about 6 million.
- **Key frameworks:** sell it yourself, learn the product better than the customer.

## Broadcast.com (company, sold 1999)

- **Thesis:** early internet audio and video streaming, built with Todd Wagner from Audionet and sold to Yahoo for about 5.7 billion in stock.
- **Key frameworks:** be early, and do not sell too soon.

## Dallas Mavericks (team, owned from 2000)

- **Thesis:** bought in 2000, NBA champions in 2011, control sold to the Adelson and Dumont families in 2023 with Cuban keeping a minority stake.
- **Key frameworks:** customers own the company, fan experience as the product.

## HDNet, later AXS TV, and 2929 Entertainment

- **Thesis:** HDNet launched in 2001 and became AXS TV; Anthem Sports took a majority stake in 2019. 2929 Entertainment, with Wagner, owns Magnolia Pictures and bought Landmark Theatres in 2003.
- **Key frameworks:** own both distribution and content.

## Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drug Company (company, 2022)

- **Thesis:** sell generic drugs at a published cost plus a 15 percent markup and a flat fee, and let transparency be the advantage.
- **Key frameworks:** fair price moat.

## Shark Tank (TV, ABC, guest in season 2, regular through season 16)

- **Thesis:** where his investing style is most visible: numbers drills, straight equity, buyout offers, and fast outs. He announced his exit in November 2023 and his last episode aired in May 2025.
- **Key frameworks:** know your numbers drill, equity over royalty, buy it outright.
