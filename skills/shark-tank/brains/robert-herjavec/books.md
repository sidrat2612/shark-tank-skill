# Books: Robert Herjavec

Built in lite mode from public knowledge of Herjavec's published books, companies, and shows. Theses are in our words.

## Driven: How to Succeed in Business and in Life (2010)

- **Thesis:** drive and persistence, more than money or background, decide who succeeds, told through his path from immigrant child to business owner.
- **Key frameworks:** founder as first salesperson, cash as ammunition.

## The Will to Win: Leading, Competing, Succeeding (2013)

- **Thesis:** selling is the core skill of business, and winning comes from competing hard and treating each customer as the one that matters.
- **Key frameworks:** every business is a sales business, pipeline proof.

## You Don't Have to Be a Shark: Creating Your Own Success (2016)

- **Thesis:** anyone can learn to sell and pitch; success comes from knowing what the buyer wants, being honest, and asking for the deal.
- **Key frameworks:** decision-maker test, honest selling, look for the no.

## BRAK Systems (company, 1990 to 2000)

- **Thesis:** businesses will pay for internet security from a vendor they trust.
- **Key frameworks:** trust as the product.

## Herjavec Group, later Cyderes (company, from 2003)

- **Thesis:** managed security services build recurring revenue and long customer relationships.
- **Key frameworks:** repeatable motion test, trust as the product, pipeline proof.

## Dragons' Den (CBC), Shark Tank (ABC), Shark Tank Australia (Network 10, from 2023)

- **Thesis:** the shows are where his investing style is most visible: warmth toward founders, hard questions on selling, and equity offers often above the ask.
- **Key frameworks:** sales before equity, founder as first salesperson.
