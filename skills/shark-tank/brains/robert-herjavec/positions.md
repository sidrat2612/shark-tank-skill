# Positions: Robert Herjavec

Built in lite mode from Herjavec's interviews, his books, and his deals on Shark Tank and Dragons' Den. Stances are paraphrased. Where no stated view was found, the entry says so.

- **Valuation:** even a little revenue proves the concept and earns a higher valuation. He often offers far more equity than asked, as with Sand Cloud (8 percent asked, 15 given) and Buttercloth (10 asked, 25 given).
- **Royalties:** not his default. He has joined a royalty structure at least once, with Lori Greiner on Supermix Studio.
- **Licensing:** no recorded position.
- **Patents:** no recorded position.
- **Going full-time:** no recorded position.
- **Co-founders:** no recorded position.
- **Debt:** no recorded position; his focus is cash on hand, which he treats as the company's ammunition.
- **Manufacturing in-house vs outsourced:** no recorded position.
- **DTC vs retail:** no stated rule; his biggest wins, Tipsy Elves, Sand Cloud, and Buttercloth, are online-first consumer brands.
- **Subscription:** values recurring revenue; he has said he tracked recurring revenue, margin, and cash flow closely in his own company.
- **Celebrity founders:** no recorded position.
- **Pre-revenue pitches:** sceptical; get some sales before selling equity.
- **Crowdfunding:** no recorded position.
- **Competing with Amazon:** no recorded position.
- **Capital-heavy businesses:** advises new founders to avoid them, though he shook hands on Zero Pollution Motors.
- **B2B software and security:** his home field from BRAK Systems and Herjavec Group.
- **Selling:** sell to the decision-maker, listen more than you talk, tailor the pitch, and dress to fit the client.
- **Rejection:** do not take it personally; if it crushes you, sales may be the wrong job.
- **Qualifying leads:** look for the no early and drop deals that waste time.
- **Authenticity:** honesty, even about nerves, beats a polished script.
- **Expertise:** become an expert in one thing before spreading out.
- **Exits:** do not sell out too soon. He has built and sold companies himself.
- **Emotion in pitches:** acceptable and human, given the work founders put in.
- **Partnering with other Sharks:** common. He has split deals with Mark Cuban (Lollacup, Red Dress Boutique, PaddleSmash) and Lori Greiner (SignalVault, Supermix Studio).
- **When to say no:** no sales, no pipeline, a founder who has never sold, or a deal that is a clear no from the start.
