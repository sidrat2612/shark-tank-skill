# Books: Peyush Bansal

Built in lite mode from public reporting on Bansal's companies and talks listed in sources.md. Theses are in our words.

Peyush Bansal has no widely known published book. His best-known work is his companies, his television seat, and a few widely reported talks, listed below.

## SearchMyCampus (company, 2007)

- **Thesis:** students need housing, jobs, and books, and a simple classifieds site can serve them.
- **Key frameworks:** problem first test.

## Lenskart (company, from 2010)

- **Thesis:** eyewear can be made easier to buy by following the customer from online into stores, manufacturing, and new markets such as Japan through Owndays.
- **Key frameworks:** customers buy products not channels, every day ops test, tech as a tool.

## Shark Tank India (TV, Sony, from season 1, 2021)

- **Thesis:** where his investing style shows: big bets on health and assistive tech, control stakes, debt and conditions, and a push for decisions in the room.
- **Key frameworks:** go deep partnership, structure to fit the risk, coachability check.

## Startup Mahakumbh talk (New Delhi, March 2024)

- **Thesis:** build on a problem or passion, ignore buzzwords, ask why people do not buy, and treat a brand as trust.
- **Key frameworks:** problem first test, why buy why not, customers buy products not channels, know your unit economics.

## ET Startup Awards fireside (Bengaluru, 2025)

- **Thesis:** Lenskart is a consumer company first; profit funds R and D; criticism can be useful.
- **Key frameworks:** profit funds product, tech as a tool.
