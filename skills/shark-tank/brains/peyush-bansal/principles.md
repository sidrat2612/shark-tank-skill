# Principles: Peyush Bansal

Built in lite mode from Bansal's public remarks and his Shark Tank India deal record listed in sources.md. Examples are paraphrased.

1. **Start with the problem.** Example: at Startup Mahakumbh 2024 he told founders to build on a problem or passion, not a model or channel.
2. **Customers buy products, not channels.** Example: he has said customers are not concerned with channels and founders should not be ruled by one.
3. **Ignore buzzwords.** Example: he has dismissed omnichannel, AI, and deep tech as labels that industries adopt and drop.
4. **Know why people do not buy.** Example: he has urged founders to dig into why customers walk away, not only why they buy.
5. **Know your unit economics.** Example: he lists understanding business economics as a must for Shark Tank founders.
6. **Be coachable.** Example: he says founders on the show should seek mentorship, not only money.
7. **Back hard problems in health and access.** Example: he invested in Annie, a braille learning device, in season 1 and in WeHear, a hearing solution, in season 3.
8. **Go big when you love it.** Example: he offered PadCare founder Ajinkya Dhariya a blank cheque in season 2.
9. **Take control when you want to build.** Example: he took 51 percent of NOOE for 5 crore in season 4, his biggest cheque on the show.
10. **Mix equity and debt.** Example: in season 1 he gave PNT Robotics 25 lakh for 25 percent plus 25 lakh as debt.
11. **Attach conditions to fix a problem first.** Example: in season 2 he offered DigiQure money on condition the founders merged their two companies.
12. **Use the deal for good.** Example: his Neomotion wheelchair deal in season 2 sent a share of profits to charity until 1 crore was donated.
13. **Ask for a decision in the room.** Example: in season 4 he withdrew his Paleoo Bakes offer when the founders took time to decide.
14. **Profit funds innovation.** Example: at the ET Startup Awards 2025 he argued profit pays for R and D that lets Indian firms match global peers.
15. **Brand is trust, not ads.** Example: he has said heavy advertising does not by itself justify a premium price.
16. **Hustle yourself.** Example: he has recalled cleaning Lenskart's first Singapore store until 1 am with his co-founders before launch.
