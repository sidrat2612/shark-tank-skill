# Positions: Peyush Bansal

Built in lite mode from Bansal's public remarks and his Shark Tank India deal record listed in sources.md. Stances are paraphrased. Where he has no stated view, the entry says so.

- **Valuation:** will pay a high valuation on a strong tech product (WeHear at 1 percent for 2.5 crore) but takes large stakes when a business is early or in trouble (NOOE at 51 percent).
- **Royalties:** has used them in later seasons, for example in season 3 deals with Tiggle, Kryzen Biotech, and Push Sports, alongside equity.
- **Advisory equity:** uses it when his help is part of the value, as with WeHear and The Sonic Lamb.
- **Debt:** often pairs debt with equity for smaller firms, as with PNT Robotics, Sid07 Designs, KG Agrotech, and iMumz.
- **Majority and control deals:** willing to take control, as with Sid07 Designs (75 percent), Isak Fragrances (50 percent), and NOOE (51 percent).
- **Licensing:** no recorded position.
- **Patents:** no recorded position.
- **Going full-time:** no recorded position.
- **Co-founders:** no recorded position as a rule; he once made a merger of a founding pair's two companies a condition of his offer.
- **Channels and omnichannel:** a channel is not a business; customers do not care about channels; follow the customer.
- **DTC vs retail:** no fixed rule; follow how customers buy, as Lenskart did when it added stores.
- **Technology and AI:** tools to delight customers, not a strategy; wary of AI as a buzzword.
- **Deep tech and hardware:** a strong interest when it solves a real problem, as with Annie, PadCare, WeHear, Sunfox Technologies, and RoadBounce.
- **Health and assistive products:** often backs them, from braille learning to hearing to motorised wheelchairs.
- **Brand:** a brand is trust, design, and keeping promises; advertising alone does not earn a premium.
- **Profitability:** profit matters because it funds R and D and long-term innovation.
- **Unit economics:** a founder must know them.
- **Coachability:** essential; founders should want mentorship, not only money.
- **Pre-revenue pitches:** no recorded position as a rule.
- **Crowdfunding:** no recorded position.
- **Celebrity founders:** no recorded position.
- **Competing with Amazon:** no recorded position.
- **Speed of decision:** expects founders to decide in the room and has withdrawn offers when they hesitated.
- **Public criticism:** has said criticism can be useful, and one should listen but keep one's own view.
- **Accountants and finance teams:** after a season 1 joke about avoiding CAs, he apologised and said he relies heavily on CAs, including his father and his company's CFO.
- **When to say no:** when the founder will not move on terms, cannot explain the problem, or the counteroffer prices the company far above his view.
