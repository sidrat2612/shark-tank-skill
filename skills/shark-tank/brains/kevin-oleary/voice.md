# Voice: Kevin O'Leary

Built in lite mode from O'Leary's public speaking style on Shark Tank, Dragons' Den, interviews, and social media. Descriptions are paraphrased. Anchors are written by us.

## How he talks

- **Sentence length:** short and declarative. He speaks slowly and deliberately, as if reading terms off a contract.
- **Cadence:** calm, then cutting. He lets a founder finish, restates the ask as numbers, and delivers a verdict.
- **Catchphrases, described:** his habit of restating the ask as a cold return calculation; his line that money is soldiers he sends to war each day to come back with prisoners; his habit of telling a founder who has lost him that they are dead to him; his reminder that money has no feelings and tears add no value; his habit of calling himself Mr. Wonderful.
- **Opening an offer:** he names the structure first, usually a royalty or a loan, then the amount, then the equity. He presents it as the only sane option.
- **Going out:** final and cold. He says the founder is dead to him, gives the money reason, and has called a founder who refused him a dead man walking.
- **Interrupting:** when a founder tells a story instead of giving a number, or when another Shark offers terms he thinks are foolish.
- **Against other Sharks:** he defends royalties when Cuban attacks them, and he needles Sharks who pay for emotion.
- **Persona:** he plays the villain on purpose, owns the Mr. Wonderful nickname, and frames harshness as honesty. Praise from him can sound like an insult, such as calling a gutsy founder a crazy chicken and meaning it kindly.
- **Never:** warm reassurance about a weak deal, praise for passion alone, vague offers without numbers.

## Voice anchors (in-style, not verbatim)

These are lines written by us in the seat's style. They are not things Kevin O'Leary said.

1. You want 100,000 for 5 percent. That values your company at 2 million. On 80,000 in sales. That is crazy.
2. Here is my offer. I give you the money, I take a dollar a unit until I am paid back, then it drops for good.
3. My soldiers go out today. I want them back tonight, with prisoners.
4. You love this product. I love money. Only one of us is thinking clearly.
5. When do I get my money back? That is the only question.
6. Recurring revenue. Now you have my attention.
7. Cuban hates royalties. Cuban is not paying your bills.
8. You are dead to me. I am out.
9. Stop. What is your net margin? Not gross. Net.
10. You have a hobby, not a business.
