# Books: Kevin O'Leary

Built in lite mode from verified public reporting on O'Leary's published writing, funds, companies, and shows. Theses are in our words.

## Cold Hard Truth: On Business, Money and Life (2011)

- **Thesis:** business is about making money, and comfort with that fact separates owners from dreamers.
- **Key frameworks:** money as soldiers, know your numbers, kill the dead deal.

## The Cold Hard Truth on Men, Women and Money (2012)

- **Thesis:** personal finance mistakes come from emotion and poor habits, and simple rules fix most of them.
- **Key frameworks:** save first, avoid bad debt, own things that pay you.

## The Cold Hard Truth on Family, Kids and Money (2015)

- **Thesis:** families should talk about money openly and teach children to save and invest early.
- **Key frameworks:** dividend discipline at home, money as a tool, not an emotion.

## The Cold Hard Truth on Marriage and Money (2015)

- **Thesis:** money is a core part of any marriage and should be planned for openly.
- **Key frameworks:** no recorded frameworks beyond his general money rules.

## Digital Pivot or Bust (2020)

- **Thesis:** after COVID-19, businesses that do not move online will not survive.
- **Key frameworks:** direct to consumer selling, online advertising as a priority.

## SoftKey and The Learning Company (company, 1986 to 1999)

- **Thesis:** grow by buying competitors and their cash flow, then sell when a buyer will pay the most.
- **Key frameworks:** valuation reality check, exit timing.

## O'Leary Funds (2008 to 2015) and O'Shares ETFs (from 2015)

- **Thesis:** own quality companies that pay you cash. Separately, he says he caps any one stock at 5 percent of a portfolio and any sector at 20 percent, a rule he credits to his mother.
- **Key frameworks:** dividend discipline, recurring revenue premium.

## Dragons' Den (CBC, 2006 to 2014), The Lang and O'Leary Exchange (CBC, from 2009), Shark Tank (ABC, from 2009)

- **Thesis:** television is where his investing style is most visible: valuation math out loud, royalties, and cold outs.
- **Key frameworks:** royalty payback rule, valuation reality check, kill the dead deal.
