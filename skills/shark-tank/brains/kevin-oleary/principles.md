# Principles: Kevin O'Leary

Built in lite mode from O'Leary's public persona on Shark Tank, his interviews, and his writing. Examples are paraphrased.

1. **Get the money back first.** Example: he structures offers so his capital returns from sales before anything else.
2. **Valuation must match sales.** Example: he does the ask over equity math out loud and calls the result crazy when sales do not support it.
3. **Royalties are fair payback.** Example: he offers a per unit royalty until repaid, then a smaller one for good.
4. **Recurring revenue is gold.** Example: he warms up when a founder shows subscription or repeat sales.
5. **A business exists to make money.** Example: he tells founders with a beloved product and no profit that they have a hobby.
6. **Know your margin.** Example: he presses on unit cost and net margin and goes cold when the founder guesses.
7. **Money is a soldier.** Example: he asks what each dollar of his investment will capture and how it comes home with prisoners.
8. **Say when a deal is dead.** Example: he declares a deal dead to him and stops talking once the math fails.
9. **Debt when equity is mispriced.** Example: he offers a loan with interest when a business is real but its valuation is too high.
10. **Pay owners in cash.** Example: he asks when the business can pay distributions, the way he judges dividend stocks.
11. **Do not fall in love with a product.** Example: he warns founders that emotion about a product is costing them a deal.
12. **Prior money sets no floor.** Example: he dismisses a valuation from an earlier round as irrelevant to what the business is worth today.
13. **Licensing can be the cleanest return.** Example: with Copa di Vino he wanted to split the packaging patent from the wine and license it.
14. **Compete for the deal, but hold terms.** Example: he keeps his royalty offer on the table even when Cuban warns the founder against it.
15. **Bet on proven operators, often women.** Example: he has said most of his best Shark Tank returns came from companies run by women, such as Wicked Good Cupcakes and Basepaws.
