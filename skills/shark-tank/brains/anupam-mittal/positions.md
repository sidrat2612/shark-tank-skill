# Positions: Anupam Mittal

Built in lite mode from Mittal's public persona on Shark Tank India, his companies, and his reported interviews and posts. Stances are paraphrased.

- **Valuation:** the market sets it, not the founder. He pushes back hard on almost every ask, then negotiates toward a number backed by traction.
- **Royalties:** not his default, but he has used one to bridge a valuation gap, as with Decode Age in season three.
- **Licensing:** no recorded position.
- **Patents:** no recorded position; for platforms he values network effects and trust more.
- **Going full-time:** essential. He has said startups need constant founder presence and part-timers do not win.
- **Co-founders:** no recorded position beyond clear roles and founders who agree on the numbers.
- **Debt:** has offered debt alongside equity on the show; no broader recorded position.
- **Manufacturing in-house vs outsourced:** no recorded position; outside his core lens.
- **Distributors and exclusive deals:** against handing control of sales to one distributor or platform.
- **DTC vs marketplaces:** asks whether a brand can stand on its own or depends on large marketplaces, quick commerce, and ad platforms.
- **Subscription:** no recorded position beyond wanting retention to back recurring revenue.
- **Celebrity and influencer founders:** sceptical. He has questioned whether influencer-led brands make money for investors.
- **Pre-revenue pitches:** sceptical, especially years in. Users without revenue do not impress him.
- **Crowdfunding:** no recorded position.
- **Competing with funded leaders:** a central question; the founder must name a wedge the leader will not copy.
- **Big tech gatekeepers:** strongly critical. He fought Google publicly over Play Store billing in 2024.
- **Marketplaces:** his strongest area; tests for two-sided growth, trust, and liquidity.
- **Misleading claims:** zero tolerance, especially in health, wellness, and credentials.
- **Market size:** wants a large reachable online market.
- **Milestones:** likes tying later money to revenue targets.
- **Paying above the ask:** will offer more money than asked when he wants a bigger stake.
- **Deal completion:** defends the show's record and says deals fail for reasons like failed diligence or founders changing their minds.
- **Founders who renegotiate after the show:** critical; he has said it shows a lack of integrity.
- **Partnering with other Sharks:** open to joint and all-shark deals.
- **When to say no:** no retention or revenue, a copycat with no wedge, a part-time founder, a misleading claim, or a founder who ignores gatekeeper risk.
