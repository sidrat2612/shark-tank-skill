# Books: Anupam Mittal

Built in lite mode from public reporting on Mittal's companies, investing, and public work. Theses are in our words.

Anupam Mittal has no widely known published book. The entries below are his best-known companies, investing, show, and public commentary, as the spec allows.

## Shaadi.com (company, from 1997)

- **Thesis:** an online matrimony platform, started as Sagai.com, that matches two sides and wins on trust, reach, and community-specific sites.
- **Key frameworks:** network effect test, trust as the product, copycat wedge check.

## People Group (company, from 1996)

- **Thesis:** the parent for his internet businesses: Shaadi.com, Makaan.com (acquired by PropTiger in 2015), and Mauj Mobile, described as India's first mobile gaming company.
- **Key frameworks:** market size sanity, platform and gatekeeper risk, network effect test.

## Angel investing (from 2007)

- **Thesis:** back Indian internet founders early. His first cheque was Interactive Avenues; Ola is the best-known bet. Business Standard ranked him a top angel investor in 2014 and 2015.
- **Key frameworks:** retention before acquisition, network effect test.

## Shark Tank India (TV, Sony and SonyLIV, Mittal as a Shark from 2021)

- **Thesis:** the show is where his investing style is most visible: hard valuation resets, retention questions, milestone tranches, and negotiating to a middle.
- **Key frameworks:** valuation reset, meet in the middle, milestone tranche, full-time founder test.

## Public posts and interviews (X, LinkedIn, press)

- **Thesis:** commentary on Indian startups, deal completion on the show, and big tech gatekeepers, including his 2024 campaign against Google's Play Store billing.
- **Key frameworks:** platform and gatekeeper risk, trust as the product.

## Films (99 and Flavors)

- **Thesis:** he acted in and produced these films, directed by his friends Raj and DK. Not a business framework source; listed for completeness.
- **Key frameworks:** none.
