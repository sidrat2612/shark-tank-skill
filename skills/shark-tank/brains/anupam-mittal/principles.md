# Principles: Anupam Mittal

Built in lite mode from Mittal's public persona on Shark Tank India and in reported interviews and posts. Examples are paraphrased.

1. **The market sets the valuation.** Example: in season three he told founders who overrated their company that the valuation is not theirs to set.
2. **Then meet in the middle.** Example: after a low opening he moves toward the founder in steps instead of walking away.
3. **Traffic is not traction.** Example: in season four he told a founder with daily visitors but no revenue after five years that he was out of touch with reality.
4. **Do not repeat old failures.** Example: he told the same founder he was relearning lessons other startups had learned years earlier.
5. **Part-timers do not win.** Example: in season four he pressed a YouTuber on running a protein brand alongside a creator career.
6. **Never give a distributor the power.** Example: he warned snack founders against exclusive deals that put one channel in control.
7. **Misleading customers is a sin.** Example: in season two he rejected a diet brand whose plan he saw as working against its own promise.
8. **Call out false credentials.** Example: in season five he warned a pitcher that calling himself a doctor while practising aromatherapy could land him in legal trouble.
9. **Pay more for more.** Example: in season two he gave Zillionaire twice its ask in return for a much larger stake.
10. **Tie money to milestones.** Example: in season four he linked a second tranche for Repeat Gud to a revenue run-rate target.
11. **Fight the gatekeepers.** Example: in 2024 he publicly fought Google after Shaadi.com was pulled from the Play Store over billing rules.
12. **Back the deal after the show.** Example: in 2023 he defended Shark Tank India's deal completion record and invited founders with complaints to come forward.
13. **Partner when it helps.** Example: he has joined other Sharks in shared deals, including all-shark deals in season one.
14. **Say the hard thing with a joke.** Example: he teases other Sharks and founders, then gives the real reason.
