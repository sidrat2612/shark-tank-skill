# Frameworks: Aman Gupta

Built in lite mode from public reporting on how Aman Gupta built boAt, his interviews, and Shark Tank India episode records. Paraphrased throughout.

## 1. Brand or product test

- **What it is:** a product is a thing people buy once. A brand is a thing people choose by name, recommend, and buy again.
- **Applied to a pitch:** ask whether a customer would notice or care if the logo were swapped for a competitor's.
- **Scoring:** a clear identity and customers who ask for the brand by name score high. A generic product with a new label scores low.
- **Source:** boAt's growth from a cable into a lifestyle audio brand; repeated on Shark Tank India.

## 2. Fix the annoyance

- **What it is:** start with a product that solves a pain customers already complain about, then expand once they trust the name.
- **Applied:** ask what everyday frustration the product removes and how customers coped before.
- **Scoring:** a sharp, common pain with a visible fix scores high. A nice-to-have with no complaint behind it scores low.
- **Source:** boAt's first product, a braided charging cable built to survive bending, made because Apple cables broke near the connector (boAt company blog, 2022).

## 3. Marketing engine check

- **What it is:** a real engine is a set of channels that bring in customers at a known cost, again and again.
- **Applied:** ask which channels work, the spend, the CAC, and how it changes as spend grows.
- **Scoring:** CAC recovered on the first order or through repeat orders scores high. Growth that stops when ads stop scores low.
- **Source:** Shark Tank India behaviour across seasons.

## 4. You are the brand

- **What it is:** in a young D2C company the founder is the face and the main marketer, so marketing cannot be outsourced.
- **Applied:** ask who makes the content, runs the ads, and talks to customers.
- **Scoring:** a founder who knows the channel numbers and fronts the brand scores well. A founder who plans to hand it all to an agency scores low.
- **Source:** Entrepreneur India interview, 2023; his role as boAt's CMO.

## 5. Frugal, return-driven marketing

- **What it is:** a low-budget founder must balance cash earned against cash burned on marketing.
- **Applied:** ask what each rupee of marketing returned and what happens if spend is cut.
- **Scoring:** measured returns and a clear payback score high. Spend justified only by brand awareness scores low.
- **Source:** Entrepreneur India interview, 2023.

## 6. Price for Bharat

- **What it is:** the largest Indian market is value-conscious, so a brand must feel premium at a price most people can pay.
- **Applied:** compare the price with what the target buyer earns and with the cheapest rivals on marketplaces.
- **Scoring:** an aspirational product at an accessible price scores high. Premium pricing without proof of repeat demand scores low.
- **Source:** boAt's positioning as trendy, affordable audio for young Indians.

## 7. Hustle first

- **What it is:** hustle is the first quality he says he looks for in a founder, because persistence is what got boAt funded after many rejections.
- **Applied:** ask what the founder did with little money and how they handled rejection.
- **Scoring:** visible, scrappy effort lifts a score even when numbers are early. Low energy is close to fatal.
- **Source:** Business Today report on his ASCENT Conclave remarks, 2022.

## 8. We could build it test

- **What it is:** if a large brand like boAt could launch the same product quickly, the startup needs a reason it still wins.
- **Applied:** ask what stops a bigger brand from copying the product next quarter.
- **Scoring:** a real edge in community, design, supply, or niche focus scores well. No answer is a serious minus.
- **Source:** his best-known running line on Shark Tank India, widely shared as a meme.

## 9. Co-invest for strength

- **What it is:** a second Shark brings skills he lacks, and a joint deal shares the risk.
- **Applied:** look at who else is interested and what they add, such as retail, health, or operations.
- **Scoring:** not a score, a structure habit. Most of his season 1 deals were joint deals.
- **Source:** Shark Tank India season records.

## 10. Equity plus debt

- **What it is:** take a smaller equity stake and put the rest in as a loan, so the founder dilutes less and the investor gets some cash back.
- **Applied:** when the ask is too rich, offer part equity and part loan at a fixed interest rate.
- **Scoring:** not a score, a structure tool. He used it with Namhya Foods in season 1, and in later seasons with loans at around 10 to 12 percent interest.
- **Source:** Shark Tank India season records; Business Today episode reports, 2024 and 2026.

## 11. Founder control

- **What it is:** heavy dilution weakens founders, and keeping a majority helps them make decisions.
- **Applied:** check the cap table and how much the founders will hold after this round.
- **Scoring:** founders who still hold a large stake score well. A cap table already split many ways is a minus.
- **Source:** Entrepreneur India interview, 2023, on boAt's founders keeping over half the company.
