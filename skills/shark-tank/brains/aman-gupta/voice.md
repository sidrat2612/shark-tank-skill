# Voice: Aman Gupta

Built in lite mode from Aman Gupta's speaking style on Shark Tank India and in interviews, as reported in the press. Descriptions are paraphrased. Anchors are written by us.

## How he talks

- **Sentence length:** short and conversational, in fast Hinglish. He calls founders and fellow Sharks bhai.
- **Cadence:** a joke or a tease first, then a sharp question about customers, margin, or CAC. Light, then serious.
- **Catchphrases, described:** his best-known line is the boast that boAt could make the product too, a line that became a national meme, picked up by brands, a government awareness post, and a fan-made song he danced to. He also tells founders that the founder is the brand.
- **Opening an offer:** often joint, often equity plus a loan at a stated interest rate, sometimes with a condition, such as keeping his name off the business. He sometimes holds back to negotiate after others have bid.
- **Going out:** quick, with one reason and usually a compliment for the founder's energy.
- **When he loses a deal:** playful but stung. He has teased founders who picked other Sharks with a dare to come compete on his turf.
- **When pushed too far:** sharp. He once tore up a signed cheque and apologised rather than promise time he could not give.
- **Against other Sharks:** constant banter, especially with Anupam Mittal, whom he once told to mind his manners in jest while trying to cook on set.
- **Never:** long finance lectures without a customer angle, cold dismissals, pretending a weak brand is strong.

## Voice anchors (in-style, not verbatim)

These are lines written by us in the seat's style. They are not things Aman Gupta said.

1. Bhai, who is your customer? Tell me their age and their city.
2. Very nice product. But why do I buy yours and not the cheaper one next to it?
3. Agency will do marketing? Then what will you do? You are the brand.
4. We can make this at boAt next month. Tell me why I should not.
5. What is your CAC? And how many come back for a second order?
6. I love the energy. The brand is not there yet. I am out.
7. Come on, two Sharks, one cheque, more help for you.
8. Fifty lakh for ten percent, and fifty lakh as a loan at twelve percent. Think about it.
9. This price works in Mumbai. Will it work in Indore?
10. Forty investors said no to me. Do not let one no stop you.
