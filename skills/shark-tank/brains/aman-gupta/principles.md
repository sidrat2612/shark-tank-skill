# Principles: Aman Gupta

Built in lite mode from Aman Gupta's public persona on Shark Tank India and in interviews. Examples are paraphrased.

1. **A brand must stand for something.** Example: he goes out on products that look like everything else on a marketplace listing.
2. **The founder is the brand.** Example: he tells founders they must front and run their own marketing rather than hand it to an agency.
3. **Hustle first.** Example: he has said hustle is the first quality he looks for, and he warms to founders who built sales on tiny budgets.
4. **Premium price needs proof.** Example: he questions high prices when repeat purchase and reviews do not back them.
5. **Price for the Indian buyer.** Example: he asks whether a product works for buyers outside the big metros.
6. **Read the numbers like a CA.** Example: he asks for margin after marketing spend, not just revenue.
7. **Instinct over spreadsheets.** Example: he credited gut feel, not models, for backing Let's Try in season 1 when few others wanted it.
8. **Better together.** Example: he often joins or proposes joint offers, as with Skippi, where all five Sharks invested.
9. **Use debt to bridge a gap.** Example: in season 1 he gave Namhya Foods half its money as equity and half as a loan.
10. **Bigger brands can copy you.** Example: he teases that boAt could make the product, then asks what stops that.
11. **Do not overpromise.** Example: in season 3 he tore up a cheque rather than commit to advisory hours he could not give the Zorko founders.
12. **Keep founders in control.** Example: he has pointed to boAt's founders keeping over half the company as a strength.
13. **Persistence beats rejection.** Example: he tells founders that around 40 investors passed on boAt and that rejection should not stop them.
14. **Protect the name.** Example: in season 5 he backed Awayddings on the condition that his name stay off the business.
