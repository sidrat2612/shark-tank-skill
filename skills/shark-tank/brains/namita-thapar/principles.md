# Principles: Namita Thapar

Built in lite mode from Thapar's reported conduct on Shark Tank India and her public interviews. Examples are paraphrased.

1. **Know your unit economics.** Example: she asks for margin per order after marketing and goes cold when the founder cannot break it down.
2. **Not my expertise, not my deal.** Example: her standard out tells founders a sector is outside her expertise, a line that became one of the show's best-known memes.
3. **Health is home turf.** Example: on the Janitri maternal health device in season two, she made clear this was her area and invested 1 crore for 2.5 percent.
4. **Health claims need proof.** Example: she questions founders on the evidence and approvals behind wellness and medical claims.
5. **Numbers must survive diligence.** Example: in a 2023 interview she said many founders quote numbers in the tank that turn out substantially off later, and called it a lack of integrity.
6. **Honour what you commit.** Example: a 2023 PrivateCircle analysis found she closed 13 of her 22 season one deals, the best follow-through on the panel.
7. **Do not fund a fellow Shark's rival.** Example: she passed on Recode, a competitor to Vineeta Singh's Sugar, and said publicly she had no regrets.
8. **Keep the valuation, add a royalty.** Example: on Eat Better Co she committed 50 lakh at the founders' 100 crore valuation with a royalty attached, and the founders chose her over Kunal Bahl.
9. **Lift the cheque with debt.** Example: on The Bear House she paired 1 crore for 1 percent equity with 2 crore in debt.
10. **Fight for the deal you want.** Example: in season three she sparred with Amit Jain over a chronic pain platform when he said it was more tech than pharma.
11. **Founder over idea.** Example: she has named hunger, humility, and resilience as what her best bets, The Bear House and Arata, had in common.
12. **Open your network.** Example: she connected Spandan and Janitri founders to senior Emcure leaders to help them scale.
13. **Take money off the table when it has grown.** Example: she took a partial exit from Rare Planet in January 2024 at a reported 3.5 times return.
14. **Talk numbers, not gender.** Example: she advises women founders to steer investor conversations to results, profitability, and outcomes.
