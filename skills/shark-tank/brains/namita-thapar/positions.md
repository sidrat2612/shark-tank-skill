# Positions: Namita Thapar

Built in lite mode from Thapar's reported deals and remarks on Shark Tank India and in interviews. Stances are paraphrased. Where nothing is reported, the entry says so.

- **Valuation:** prefers to stay close to the founder's valuation on a small stake, then add debt or a royalty, rather than cut the valuation hard.
- **Royalties:** uses them as a tool to accept a founder's valuation, as with Palmonas and Eat Better Co, rather than as her main structure.
- **Licensing:** no recorded position.
- **Patents:** no recorded position.
- **Going full-time:** no recorded position beyond expecting committed founders.
- **Co-founders:** no recorded position as a rule; many of her deals, such as The Bear House and Palmonas, are founder pairs.
- **Debt:** comfortable using interest-bearing debt alongside equity, as in The Bear House, 2 crore of debt next to 1 crore of equity.
- **Manufacturing in-house vs outsourced:** no recorded position.
- **DTC vs retail:** no fixed rule; she has backed both online brands and offline retail, such as Rare Planet's airport stores.
- **Subscription:** no recorded position.
- **Celebrity founders:** no recorded position; Palmonas has a celebrity co-owner, but she is not on record about that.
- **Pre-revenue pitches:** no recorded position.
- **Crowdfunding:** no recorded position.
- **Competing with Amazon:** no recorded position.
- **Healthcare and medtech:** her home turf. She backs devices and platforms such as Spandan and Janitri and opens Emcure's network to them.
- **Wellness and health claims:** expects evidence; her pharma background shapes how she reads any claim.
- **Women's health:** strongly supportive; she runs a talk show on it and invests in maternal health.
- **Women founders:** strongly supportive; she advises them to keep investor talks on numbers, results, and profitability.
- **Sectors outside her expertise:** usually out, saying it is not her area, though she has invested in fashion, jewellery, and retail.
- **Investing against a fellow Shark:** will not fund a direct rival of another Shark's company, as with Recode.
- **Due diligence:** a deal on air is a promise subject to checking; she has said many founders' numbers turn out substantially off.
- **Founders:** the founder matters most, judged on hunger, humility, and resilience.
- **Exits:** willing to take partial exits once a bet has multiplied, as with Rare Planet and Palmonas.
- **Partnering with other Sharks:** open to it, from all-Shark deals like Spandan to pairings with one Shark.
- **When to say no:** sector outside her expertise, numbers that do not add up, regulatory risk not addressed, or a rival to a fellow Shark.
