# Books: Namita Thapar

Built in lite mode from public knowledge of Thapar's published writing, her shows, and her companies. Theses are in our words.

## The Dolphin and the Shark: Stories on Entrepreneurship (2022)

- **Thesis:** published by Penguin Business in fifteen chapters. Leaders today must balance the shark's aggression with the dolphin's empathy. Draws on Emcure's history, entrepreneurs who inspired her, and season one pitches.
- **Key frameworks:** dolphin and shark balance, founder first, unit economics stack.

## Emcure Pharmaceuticals (company; CFO, then Executive Director)

- **Thesis:** a Pune-based pharmaceutical company led by her father Satish Mehta, built through process and compliance, listed on the Indian exchanges in July 2024. She runs the India business.
- **Key frameworks:** governance check, regulatory readiness, health claim evidence test.

## Incredible Ventures youth entrepreneurship academy (company, around 2017)

- **Thesis:** entrepreneurship can be taught to students aged 11 to 18 through structured problem solving and comfort with failure. Sources name the programme variously, including Young Entrepreneurs Academy and Thapar Entrepreneurs Academy.
- **Key frameworks:** founder first.

## Uncondition Yourself with Namita (YouTube talk show, from the pandemic years)

- **Thesis:** plain-language conversations on women's health to break stigma, prompted by India's very low World Economic Forum ranking on women's health.
- **Key frameworks:** health claim evidence test.

## Shark Tank India (TV, Sony, Thapar as a Shark from season one, 2021)

- **Thesis:** the show is where her investing style is most visible: number questions, compliance checks, small stakes with debt or royalty, and fast outs outside her sectors.
- **Key frameworks:** circle of competence, valuation sanity, equity plus debt.
