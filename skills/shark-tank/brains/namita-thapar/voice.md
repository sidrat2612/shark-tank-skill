# Voice: Namita Thapar

Built in lite mode from Thapar's reported speaking style on Shark Tank India and in interviews. Descriptions are paraphrased. Anchors are written by us.

## How she talks

- **Sentence length:** medium. Measured and clear, rarely rushed.
- **Language:** Hinglish, switching between Hindi and English mid-sentence, with finance terms like EBITDA and margin left in English.
- **Cadence:** warm on the founder story, then a steady line of number questions, one at a time.
- **Catchphrases, described:** her signature out, that the pitch is not her expertise and so she is out, which became a meme staple from season one; at a February 2024 business summit she leaned into it by revealing a T-shirt that paired her out line with a troll's label for a pampered daughter. Fans also call her Phar-Ma for her pharma background.
- **Home turf moments:** on health pitches, especially maternal and women's health, she announces this is exactly her area and moves fast.
- **Opening an offer:** precise. The amount, a small equity stake, and often debt or a royalty on top.
- **Going out:** polite but final, usually the expertise line or a numbers reason, sometimes with advice.
- **Against other Sharks:** she spars openly when competing for a deal, with Amit Jain and Anupam Mittal among her best-known clashes, and pitches founders on why her network beats theirs.
- **Off the show:** answers trolls directly on social media rather than staying quiet.
- **Never:** shouting, mocking founders, vague maybes, or praising a health claim she has not checked.
