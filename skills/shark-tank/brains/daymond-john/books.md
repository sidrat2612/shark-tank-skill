# Books: Daymond John

Built in lite mode from public knowledge of John's published books and companies, checked against sources.md. Theses are in our words.

## Display of Power (2007)

- **Thesis:** his own story of building FUBU from a house in Queens into a global brand, written with Daniel Paisner.
- **Key frameworks:** sold it yourself test, manufacturing path check, authentic community, cost to make and price to sell.

## The Brand Within (2010)

- **Thesis:** everyone and everything is a brand, and a brand is the emotional connection people have with a name. It also covers what went wrong when FUBU had too much product.
- **Key frameworks:** brand or product test, licensing lens.

## The Power of Broke (2016)

- **Thesis:** having little money is an advantage, because it forces creativity, hustle, and closeness to the customer. It won an NAACP Image Award.
- **Key frameworks:** power of broke, SHARK points, goals on paper.

## Rise and Grind (2018)

- **Thesis:** the daily habits, routines, and persistence of successful people, drawn from his own life and interviews with others.
- **Key frameworks:** goals on paper, keep swimming.

## Powershift (2020)

- **Thesis:** influence, win-win negotiation, and lasting relationships decide outcomes in business and life. Written with Daniel Paisner.
- **Key frameworks:** influence, negotiation, relationships; people first.

## Little Daymond Learns to Earn (2023)

- **Thesis:** a children's book teaching young readers about earning and starting a business.
- **Key frameworks:** none used in the tank.

## FUBU (company, from 1992)

- **Thesis:** a brand born inside hip-hop culture, made by and for that community, grown through licensing, then hurt by overproduction.
- **Key frameworks:** authentic community, licensing lens.

## The Shark Group (company)

- **Thesis:** a brand consulting and marketing firm he founded and leads, which applies his brand-building playbook to other companies and celebrities.
- **Key frameworks:** brand or product test.

## Shark Tank (TV, ABC, John as a Shark from 2009)

- **Thesis:** the show is where his investing style is most visible: cost questions, brand reads, people-first judgements, and equity offers tied to manufacturing and licensing help.
- **Key frameworks:** cost to make and price to sell, manufacturing path check, people first.
