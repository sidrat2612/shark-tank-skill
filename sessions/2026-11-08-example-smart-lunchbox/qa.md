# Questions and answers (no-questions mode)

The entrepreneur was not available. Each Shark stated the question it would have asked and the answer it assumed. Deduped, hardest first, with attribution.

1. **What does it really cost to land one unit, all in?** (Lori, Daymond, Mr. Wonderful)
   Assumed: the 31 is the factory price. Daymond assumes landed is about 36; Lori assumes 31 with a 41 wholesale price. Either way, a retail unit leaves the company 5 to 10.
2. **What does the sensor and app cost to run per unit per year, and is there a subscription?** (Cuban, Mr. Wonderful)
   Assumed: a few dollars a unit, not tracked, no subscription, and app use drops after the first month.
3. **What stops a big lunchbox brand from adding a cheap sensor next season?** (Cuban, Lori)
   Assumed: only the provisional patent on the latch, which a rival can design around.
4. **What were real sales after retailer units, and what is the net margin?** (Mr. Wonderful, Cuban)
   Assumed: about 140,000 in blended revenue, net close to breakeven. That puts the ask at about 18 times sales.
5. **What does a website customer cost to acquire?** (Mr. Wonderful)
   Assumed: 20 to 30 in paid ads, leaving single digits per direct sale.
6. **Who is part-time, and when do they go full-time?** (Barbara)
   Assumed: the part-time founder keeps a day job until funding lands and will not commit to a date.
7. **Who makes it, at what minimums, and is there a second source?** (Daymond, Lori)
   Assumed: one overseas factory for the box, another supplier for the sensor, no second source, never run a 10,000 unit order.
8. **Do you own the name?** (Daymond)
   Assumed: no trademark.

Also assumed: the money goes mostly to back-to-school inventory (Mr. Wonderful); the full-time founder sold the first units through friends and parent groups (Barbara).
