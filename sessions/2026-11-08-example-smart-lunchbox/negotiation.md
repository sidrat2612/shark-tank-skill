# Round 3: no negotiation (all out)

Every Shark went out in Round 2, so the all-out override applies: no counter cycles. Each Shark answered one question instead: what would have made you say yes? Cards saved verbatim.

## Mr. Wonderful

SHARK: MR. WONDERFUL
WHAT WOULD HAVE MADE ME SAY YES: A net of 15 dollars or more per box, all in, and you prove it with a factory quote showing landed cost under 25 at 10,000 units. Or a paid app subscription that turns a box bought once a year into money that comes back every month. Then I write the 250,000 as a per-unit royalty until I'm repaid, plus low equity, and my soldiers have a road home. (Margin first, Royalty payback rule)
FINAL INVESTABILITY: 2/10
FINAL PILLAR SCORE: 2/10
