# Pitch brief: Smart lunchbox

Mode: full tank, no-questions (unattended example run). Currency: USD.

## 1. The company and the product

An insulated school lunchbox with a small app-connected sensor that reports food temperature and whether the child ate. The problem: parents of school kids cannot tell if lunch stayed safe or got eaten.

## 2. The ask

250,000 for 10%. Implied valuation: 250,000 / 0.10 = **2,500,000**.

## 3. Traction

- 2,400 units sold in 11 months.
- Revenue at most 165,600 (2,400 x 69) if every unit sold at retail. Some units went through retailers at wholesale, so real revenue is lower (assumed).
- Channels: mostly their own website, plus two regional retailers.
- This year's projection: not given. Repeat rate: not given (a lunchbox is a once-a-year purchase per child, assumed).

## 4. Unit economics

- Retail price 69. Cost to make 31.
- Gross margin at retail: (69 - 31) / 69 = 55%.
- Wholesale price to retailers: 41 (assumed), gross margin 24% (assumed).
- CAC: not given. App and sensor running costs: not given.

## 5. Team and founder

Two co-founders, one full-time, one part-time. Money invested by the founders: not given. The part-time founder's day job: not given.

## 6. Protection and moat

Provisional patent on the sensor latch. No trademark, data, or supply exclusivity mentioned.

## 7. Use of funds

Not given. Assumed: inventory for the next school season, marketing, and app work (assumed).
